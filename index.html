Te-Puia

A place that changes you.

About

Te Puia, located in Rotorua, New Zealand, is a cultural and geothermal destination that showcases both Maori traditions and the natural wonders of the Te Whakarewarewa geothermal valley. It's home to the New Zealand Maori Arts and Crafts Institute, where visitors can witness traditional carving and weaving, and it also features the famous Pōhutu Geyser, bubbling mud pools, and native kiwi birds.

Te Puia, specifically the New Zealand Māori Arts and Crafts Institute within it, has been around since 1963, when it was established by the NZ Māori Arts & Crafts Institute Act. This makes it 62 years old in 2025. It was created to preserve and promote Māori arts and culture.

What we do

Te Puia does many things, but its core function is to showcase the connection between New Zealand's natural geothermal wonders and Māori culture.

It is a tourist attraction and a cultural center located in Rotorua's Te Whakarewarewa Thermal Valley, offering guided tours of geothermal features like geysers and mud pools, opportunities to see the national kiwi bird, and cultural experiences including Māori Arts and Crafts Institute training in carving and weaving, and traditional performances.

Featuring dramatic geysers, bubbling mud, and beautiful native bush explored by our personalised guided tours. Te Puia is home to the New Zealand Māori Arts and Crafts Institute and offers a state-of-the-art Kiwi Conservation Centre, so you can get closer than ever before to New Zealands national treasure- the native Kiwi bird.

For generations, Te Puia has welcomed manuhiri (visitors) from all over the world to explore the magic hidden within Te Whakarewarewa geothermal valley during both the day and evening. Nau mai, haere mai. We look forward to welcoming you.
